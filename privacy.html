Privacy policy

Hetal Shah Personal PC Upload is a private tool used only by its owner, Dr. Hetal Shah, with the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: files in the owner's Google Drive and messages in the owner's Gmail, only when the owner runs it.
  • What it does with them: copies and organizes the owner's own files. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
  • Where data stays: in the owner's own Google Drive and on the owner's own computer.
  • How to stop it: remove its access at myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.
